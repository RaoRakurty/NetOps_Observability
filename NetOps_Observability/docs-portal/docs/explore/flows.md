# Flow analytics

Once devices are exporting **[flow records](/send-data/flows)**, analyze traffic at <kbd>Flows</kbd>.

## What you can see

- **Top talkers** — the heaviest sources/destinations by volume.
- **Conversations** — who talked to whom.
- **Services / ports** — the busiest services.
- **Application attribution** — flows resolved to real applications (cloud/SaaS ranges, DNS correlation, and on‑box firewall App‑ID), with an honest "unknown" bucket rather than false labels.

## Tips

- On sampled flows (sFlow), volumes are scaled by the sampling rate.
- Use flow context alongside **[incidents](/incidents/overview)** — a traffic spike aligning with an incident is corroborating evidence.
