# Device Geomap

The **Device Geomap** places your devices and sites on a world map, so distributed and multi‑site networks are easy to reason about geographically. Open it at <kbd>Infrastructure → Device Geomap</kbd>.

## Get devices onto the map

Devices appear where their **site** has a location. To populate the map:

1. Assign devices to **sites** (<kbd>Infrastructure → Devices</kbd>, or via the [Source of Truth](/automation/overview)).
2. Give sites a location (coordinates or address).

Site health rolls up onto the map, so you can spot a struggling location at a glance and drill in.
