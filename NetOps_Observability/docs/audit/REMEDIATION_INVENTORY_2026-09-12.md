## How to regenerate this document

    python3 docs/audit/gen-remediation-inventory.py > docs/audit/REMEDIATION_INVENTORY_2026-09-12.md

It reads its inputs from the same directory — `disposition-overrides.json`
(the verdicts, one per finding), `inventory.json`, `triage-results.json`,
`reflag-results.json` and `section2-inventory.json` — plus the branch's own
git log. Re-run it after any change to a disposition so the document and the
tracker cannot drift apart — and when they do disagree, `docs/TRACKER.md` is
the authority and this document is the stale one.
