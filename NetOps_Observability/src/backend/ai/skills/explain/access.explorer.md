---
topic: access.explorer
question: What does the Access Explorer show?
keywords: access explorer, why does this account have this access, what can this person reach, explain access
---
It answers "why does this account have this access?". Type a person, service
or agent and the page lists every tenant they can act in, and beside each one
the exact binding that grants it — the role, the scope, who granted it, and
whether it expires. A deny is shown in red and wins over any allow; a
break-glass grant is flagged. Leave the field blank to explain your own
access. This is a read-only view of the decider the server actually uses, so
what it shows is what will happen, not a summary of policy.
