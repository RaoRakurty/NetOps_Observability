---
topic: access.org
question: Who has access to this organization?
keywords: org access, assign access, role in this organization
---
The people who hold a role in this organization, and nothing else. Assigning
access here gives an existing user a role with the scope already fixed to this
organization, so you cannot accidentally grant it somewhere else. Revoking
takes effect at once. The same bindings appear on the platform-wide Assign
access screen, where every scope is listed together.
