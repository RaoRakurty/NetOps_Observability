---
topic: admin.discovery
question: What does the Discovery page set up?
keywords: discovery, network scan, snmp profiles, sensors, collection coverage, telemetry coverage, find devices
---
Everything that makes devices and their data appear, in setup order. SNMP
Profiles hold the credentials. Network Scan finds devices on address ranges
and binds each to the profile that answered. Sensors shows which collectors
run and turns SNMP, gNMI, NETCONF and trap collection on or off — platform
administrators only; most switches apply after a restart. Collection Coverage
shows what each device is collected for, and Telemetry Coverage what the
parsers recognize. Open a section to load it; closed sections cost nothing.
