---
topic: admin.identity-tree
question: How is Identity and Access organised?
keywords: identity tree, organizations tenants users, provider realm
---
One tree. Organizations sit at the top; each organization holds its optional
tenants, and people live under whichever organization or tenant they belong
to. Open a row to manage that scope's users, access, roles, security settings
and tenants. The Provider row is the platform's own realm — the accounts that
run the platform itself, rather than any customer. The single "＋ Add" button
is the guided way to create an organization, a tenant, a user or an access
grant; nothing is created until you finish it.
