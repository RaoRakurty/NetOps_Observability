---
topic: admin.identity
question: What does Identity and Access cover?
keywords: identity and access, users roles security, tenant identity
---
Your tenant's own people and the rules that apply to them: the user directory,
the roles those users hold, the external SSO role mappings, and the scope-wide
security settings for passwords, lockout and sessions. Everything on this page
is scoped to your tenant — you cannot see or change another tenant's users
from here.
