question: What does an integration connect?
keywords: integrations, system of record, servicenow jira, connector
---
Your system of record. A connector holds the connection to ServiceNow or Jira,
the routing that decides which incidents cut a ticket, and the direction state
travels. Setting one up is one guided flow: connect, route, sync, review.
Secrets are write-only — a blank field keeps what is already stored. Saving
takes effect immediately; nothing restarts.
