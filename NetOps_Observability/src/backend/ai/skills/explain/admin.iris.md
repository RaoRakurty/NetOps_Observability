topic: admin.iris
question: What is on the Administration → Iris page?
keywords: iris administration, iris settings page, ai models tab, iris knowledge tab
---
Everything an administrator sets up for Iris, on four tabs. AI models: the
model Iris uses, its key and a connection test, which workspaces may use it
and their limits, and Iris in Slack and Teams. Knowledge: facts about your
network, the names your team uses, and your runbooks. Learning & review:
proposals Iris learned from feedback, recent questions, how Iris decided and
what it could not answer. Investigations: investigations Iris runs on its own
and actions it proposes. Facts, runbooks and learning proposals still need a
second administrator to approve them, and each workspace sees only its own.
