---
topic: admin.organizations
question: What is an organization here?
keywords: organization, customer account, business unit, org
---
An organization is the top-level customer or business-unit account. Its data
region and its sign-in connection are set once on the organization and
inherited by every tenant under it. Open one to manage its users, tenants,
roles and security settings. The Provider organization is the platform's own
account and cannot be deleted; a customer organization that still holds
tenants cannot be deleted either — move or remove its tenants first.
