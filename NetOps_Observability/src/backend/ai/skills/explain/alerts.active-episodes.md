---
topic: alerts.active-episodes
question: What is an alert episode?
keywords: alert episode, active episodes, episode
---
An episode is one condition on one resource, from the moment it starts firing
until it has stayed quiet past its close window. Every repeat firing in
between joins the same episode and raises its occurrence count. Active
episodes counts the ones firing now. Triaging an episode once therefore covers
every repeat of it, which is the point.
