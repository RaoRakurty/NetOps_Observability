---
topic: alerts.critical
question: What makes an alert critical?
keywords: critical alert, critical severity alert
---
Critical on Active Alerts counts episodes whose condition was declared severe
by the rule that raised it — the monitor's own severity, not a judgement made
afterwards. It says the condition matters, not that customer impact has been
proven; proof is what correlation and RCA add on the Command Center. Edit the
severity where the rule is defined, under Monitor Rules.
