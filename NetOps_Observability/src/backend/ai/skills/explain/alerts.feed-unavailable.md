---
topic: alerts.feed-unavailable
question: The alert feed is unavailable — is that an all-clear?
keywords: alert feed unavailable, feed failed, alerts unknown
---
No. When the alert API does not answer, whether anything is firing is unknown.
The page shows an em dash rather than a zero for every count, because a green
zero would be a claim about your network that nothing measured. Rows still on
screen are the last successful read and may be stale. The condition to fix is
the platform's, not the network's.
