---
topic: alerts.flapping
question: What does Flapping mean?
keywords: flapping, flap, rapid state changes
---
Flapping on Active Alerts marks an episode whose condition has crossed its
threshold repeatedly in a short period. It usually means an unstable link or a
threshold set too close to normal behaviour, rather than a problem that starts
and stops. The flip count is on the episode. Treat the instability as the
fault to fix, not each flip.
