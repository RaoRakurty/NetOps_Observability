---
topic: alerts.nothing-firing
question: Nothing is firing — what does that prove?
keywords: nothing firing, no alerts, all clear
---
An empty Active Alerts list means every monitored condition is within its
threshold right now. It proves nothing about conditions nobody monitors: a
silent page and an unmonitored fault look the same. Monitor Rules lists what
is actually being evaluated, and Data Sources shows whether the telemetry
behind those rules is arriving.
