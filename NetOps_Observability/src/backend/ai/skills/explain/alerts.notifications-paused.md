---
topic: alerts.notifications-paused
question: What happens when notifications are paused?
keywords: notifications paused, muted, snoozed
---
Pausing notifications on an episode stops it sending — mute is open-ended,
snooze expires at a time you set. Nothing else changes: the episode stays in
the list, keeps counting occurrences and keeps updating, so a paused alert is
never a hidden one. Who muted it, or when the snooze ends, is shown on the
episode itself.
