---
topic: aspa.draft-status
question: How much should I trust the approved-provider list?
keywords: aspa, approved providers, ietf draft, do not alert, as provider authorization
---
ASPA — AS Provider Authorization — is the AS holder's published list of carriers
allowed to carry their routes. In deployment terms it is still an IETF draft:
few holders publish it, and validator coverage is thin. So read it as
corroboration and do not alert on it. There is also no public per-ASN ASPA API,
which is why this card says "no source configured" until you point it at your
own validator rather than inventing a verdict.
