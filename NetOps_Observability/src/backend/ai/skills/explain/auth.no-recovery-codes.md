---
topic: auth.no-recovery-codes
question: What happens if I lose the device with my codes?
keywords: recovery codes, lost phone, lost device, locked out, reset two-factor
---
This platform issues no recovery codes. There is nothing to print and put in a
drawer, so do not go looking for a list you were never given. If the device
holding your codes is lost, the only way back in is an administrator resetting
two-factor for your account — a person, not a self-service page. Plan for that
before you enrol: know who your administrator is, and consider enrolling the
authenticator app on a device you can still reach. Once the reset is done you
enrol again from this card, with a fresh secret; the old one is dead.
