---
topic: backup.read-only
question: Why can I not take or restore a copy?
keywords: read only posture, platform administrator, backup permissions
---
Backup and recovery is platform-global configuration, not per-tenant data, so
taking, restoring, verifying and deleting restore points requires a platform
administrator. Everyone with access to the page can read the posture; the
controls are hidden rather than offered and then refused. Ask a platform
administrator to run the action, or to grant the role.
