---
topic: backup.recovery-point
question: What is the recovery point per engine?
keywords: recovery point, rpo, objective met, objective missed, achieved recovery point
---
The recovery point is how much data you would lose if you restored right now:
the age of the newest good copy for that engine. Where an objective is set, the
row says whether the achieved point meets it. Where none is set, the age is
shown on its own and labelled as an achievement without an objective — the
platform will not invent a target you did not choose. An engine with no
measurement says so rather than showing a zero.
