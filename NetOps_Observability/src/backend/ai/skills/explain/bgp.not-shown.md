topic: bgp.not-shown
question: What does the BGP screen deliberately not show?
keywords: irr route object, looking glass, third party corroboration, bgp gaps, not shown
---
Three things are named as absent on BGP Operations rather than left as an empty
box that reads clean. IRR route-object consistency is not checked because no IRR
mirror is built here. On-demand looking-glass verification is not wired. Neither
is third-party corroboration from other measurement feeds. They are absent, not
empty: nothing on this page has looked at them, so their silence is no evidence
of health. The BGP capability tracker holds the plan for each.
