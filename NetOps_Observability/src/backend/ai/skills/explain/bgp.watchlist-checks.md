---
topic: bgp.watchlist-checks
question: What does watching a prefix actually do?
keywords: watchlist, prefixes watched, automatic checks, re-checked automatically
---
A watched prefix or AS is re-evaluated on its own schedule and can raise an
alert without anyone opening this page. An unwatched one is only ever checked
at the moment you look it up here. When automatic checks are off, the watchlist
is still stored but nothing is being evaluated between visits, so a quiet screen
is not proof of a quiet network. The caption under the tile says which of the
two states you are in.
