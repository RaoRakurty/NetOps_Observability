question: What is in the Control-plane section?
keywords: control plane section, bgp ospf convergence
---
Control-plane shows whether routing is converged and stable: BGP peers and
OSPF neighbours, with the sessions that are not established called out. A
session flapping in and out matters as much as one that is down — traffic
reroutes each time. Full protocol detail lives under Investigate.
