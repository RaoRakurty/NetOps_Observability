topic: board.errors-quality
question: What is in Errors & quality?
keywords: errors and quality, interface errors, discards
---
Errors & quality shows where a link is taking damage: interfaces counting
errors or CRCs, and interfaces discarding packets. Errors usually point at the
physical layer — optics, cabling, duplex; discards usually point at congestion
or a policy dropping traffic on purpose. Both degrade an application long
before the interface goes down.
