topic: board.topology
question: What does the Topology section show?
keywords: topology section, how it is wired
---
Topology shows how the estate is wired together, built from what the platform
has actually observed — neighbour discovery, routing adjacencies and flows —
not from a drawing kept by hand. That is why it can differ from your
documentation, and why the difference is usually worth investigating. The full
canvas lives under Investigate.
