---
topic: board.traffic-flows
question: What is in Traffic & flows?
keywords: traffic and flows, flow data, top talkers
---
Traffic & flows shows what is actually moving: total throughput, the busiest
hosts, the protocol mix, tunnel health and the vendor spread of the devices
reporting. It comes from flow records, so it answers who is talking to whom —
a different question from the interface counters in the WAN section, which
answer how loaded a link is.
