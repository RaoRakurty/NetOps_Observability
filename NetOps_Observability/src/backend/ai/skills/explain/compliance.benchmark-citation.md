---
topic: compliance.benchmark-citation
question: What is a benchmark reference?
keywords: benchmark citation, cis benchmark section, published benchmark
---
A device benchmark — a published CIS benchmark and its section heading — is a
citation on a control row: it says which published guidance the check
implements, with its title, version and section. It is never a framework of its
own. A control with no citation is still checked; the platform simply has no
published section to point at for it.
