---
topic: compliance.not-certified
question: Is this a compliance certification?
keywords: not certified compliance, audit-ready evidence, framework score meaning
---
No. These scores are audit-ready control evidence mapped to framework controls
— not certified compliance. They say what a configuration audit of this estate
can evidence right now, against the controls the platform has checks for.
Certification is an assessor's judgement over a much wider scope: process,
people, physical controls and systems this platform never sees. Use these
scores to find gaps and to hand an assessor evidence, never to claim an
outcome.
