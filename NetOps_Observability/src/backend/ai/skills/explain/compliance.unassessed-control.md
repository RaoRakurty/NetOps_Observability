---
topic: compliance.unassessed-control
question: How are unassessed controls counted?
keywords: unassessed control, no verdict, not counted as passing, unknown not compliant
---
They are counted in no passing share. An unassessed control is unknown, not
compliant: the check did not apply, could not run, or errored, so nothing is
known about it. Including it as a pass would inflate every framework score by
exactly the amount nobody measured. The panel groups them by the reason they
reached no verdict — fix the reason and the next scan produces a real one.
