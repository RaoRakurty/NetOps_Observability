---
topic: dem.absence-not-health
question: Is an empty measurement the same as a healthy one?
keywords: absent measurement, empty grid, absence not health, nothing measured
---
No, and Digital Experience never renders it that way. Where a number could not
be made, the reason it could not is shown in its place. An empty heatmap,
timeline or incident list means nothing was measured in that window — which is
a real result only when the telemetry behind it is flowing. The Data Health
tab is where that is decided.
