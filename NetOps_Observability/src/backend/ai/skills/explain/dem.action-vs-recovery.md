---
topic: dem.action-vs-recovery
question: Does a completed action mean the service recovered?
keywords: action completed, verified recovery, recovery confirmation
---
No. An action completing is a fact about the action. Digital Experience
records recovery only when the measurements agree that experience came back —
the same checks that detected the incident, read after the change. That is why
this panel can show an action done and recovery not confirmed at the same
time.
