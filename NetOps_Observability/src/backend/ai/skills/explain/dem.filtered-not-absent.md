---
topic: dem.filtered-not-absent
question: This list is filtered — what am I not seeing?
keywords: filtered list, narrowed list, hidden by a filter
---
A filter narrows what is shown; it does not change what exists. On Digital
Experience an incident hidden by a severity, application or journey filter is
still open. Clear the filters before reading an empty list as quiet — what is
hidden by a filter and what is genuinely absent look identical on screen and
mean opposite things.
