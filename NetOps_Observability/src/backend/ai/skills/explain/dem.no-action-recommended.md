---
topic: dem.no-action-recommended
question: Why is nothing recommended?
keywords: no recommended action, nothing recommended, action empty
---
Digital Experience only offers an action it can ground in the evidence shown
above it. When the evidence does not support one, none is offered rather than
a plausible-sounding guess. Work from the hypotheses and the missing evidence
instead: closing a collection gap is often what turns this panel into a real
recommendation.
