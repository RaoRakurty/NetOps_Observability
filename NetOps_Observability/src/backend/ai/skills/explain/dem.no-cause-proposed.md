---
topic: dem.no-cause-proposed
question: Why was no cause proposed?
keywords: no cause proposed, no hypothesis, hypotheses empty
---
No hypothesis means the evidence gathered for this incident did not implicate
anything strongly enough to name. It does not mean nothing is wrong — the
incident exists because something was measured. It usually points at a gap in
what was collected during the window; the Missing evidence view lists the
sources that could have produced a cause and did not report.
