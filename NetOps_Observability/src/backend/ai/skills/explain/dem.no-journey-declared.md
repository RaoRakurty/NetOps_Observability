---
topic: dem.no-journey-declared
question: Why is no journey shown?
keywords: no journey declared, journeys empty, declare a journey
---
A journey is a workflow you describe — the ordered steps a user takes and what
measures each one. Digital Experience will not invent one: a workflow nobody
described cannot be reported on. Declare a journey on the Journeys tab and its
success rate, latency objective and coverage gaps start being tracked from
that point.
