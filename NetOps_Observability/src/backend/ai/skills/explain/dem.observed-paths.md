---
topic: dem.observed-paths
question: Where do service paths come from?
keywords: service path, observed path, path hops
---
A path is shown only where one was observed. The ordered hops come from the
service path graph and are fetched by the observation reference on the
incident — they are never reconstructed from whatever evidence happened to be
collected. That is why a path can be absent for an incident that is otherwise
well evidenced.
