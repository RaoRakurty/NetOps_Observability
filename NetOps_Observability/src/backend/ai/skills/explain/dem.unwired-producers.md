---
topic: dem.unwired-producers
question: Nothing changed — or nothing was reported?
keywords: change feed empty, no change recorded, unwired producers
---
Only the change producers you have wired report into Digital Experience —
configuration backups, deployment hooks, ticket systems. An empty change feed
therefore means nothing was reported in the window, not that nothing changed.
Where change correlation matters to you, check which producers are connected
before treating the absence as evidence.
