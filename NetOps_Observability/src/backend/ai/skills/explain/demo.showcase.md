question: What is on the showcase board?
keywords: showcase, demo board, live network intelligence, marketing board, what feeds this
---
Everything on this board is read from the running stack — nothing is faked or
replayed. The panels are drawn from SNMP device telemetry, NetFlow traffic
records, synthetic probe results and the correlation engine's root-cause
verdicts, all on one screen. The "Live" state comes from real fetch outcomes,
so if the stack stops answering the board says so rather than pretending. It is
a re-styling of the same numbers the operational boards use, not a second
source of truth.
