---
topic: devices.down
question: What does Down mean for a device?
keywords: device down, no heartbeat
---
Down means no heartbeat has arrived within the expected interval. It is what
the platform can see, which is not always what happened: a device is also Down
when the path to it fails, when its collection credentials stop working, or
when the collector itself is unhealthy. The device's detail view separates
those causes.
