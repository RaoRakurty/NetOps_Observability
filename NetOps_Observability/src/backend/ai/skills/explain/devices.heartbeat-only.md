---
topic: devices.heartbeat-only
question: Alert correlation is unavailable — what changes?
keywords: heartbeat only, alert correlation unavailable, alert state unknown
---
When alert correlation cannot be read, device health falls back to the
heartbeat alone. A device shown as Up is then only known to be answering; it
may still be alerting. Nothing is hidden and nothing is invented — the page
states the reduced basis instead of quietly weakening the meaning of Up. The
reason is shown beside the warning.
