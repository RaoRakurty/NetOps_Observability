---
topic: devices.monitored
question: What does Monitored count?
keywords: monitored devices, licensed devices
---
Monitored counts the devices Correlix collects from: every device in the
inventory that has a management address, up to your licence limit, first found
first. There is no per-device switch. Devices past the limit stay listed,
marked over the licence limit, and start being collected from when a device is
deleted or the licence grows.
