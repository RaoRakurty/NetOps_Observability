---
topic: devices.up
question: What does Up mean for a device?
keywords: device up, healthy device, fresh heartbeat
---
Up means the device's heartbeat is fresh and no alert is correlated against
it. Both halves matter: a device answering on time can still be alerting, and
this state says it is not. If alert correlation is unavailable the page says
so rather than continuing to call devices Up on the heartbeat alone.
