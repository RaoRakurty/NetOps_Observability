---
topic: exposures.evidence-lane
question: Why can I not filter by lane?
keywords: evidence lane breakdown, read-only facet, cannot filter by lane
---
The evidence lane list is a breakdown of what the current filter set already
contains, not a control: the findings query has no lane filter, so making the
rows clickable would promise a narrowing the platform cannot perform. A control
that changes nothing is a lie about what the screen is showing. To see the
threat lane on its own, open Threat Detection.
