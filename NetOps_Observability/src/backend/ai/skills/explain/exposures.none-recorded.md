---
topic: exposures.none-recorded
question: What does an empty findings list prove?
keywords: no findings recorded, empty list, nothing assessed, estate clear
---
Nothing has been recorded for this tenant, which means nothing was assessed —
not that the estate is clear. The two look identical on a list and are opposite
facts. Check the Security lane panel on the overview: if it has never run, or
its findings-published counter is zero, the list is empty because nobody
looked. If a filter is applied, clear it before reading the emptiness as a
result.
