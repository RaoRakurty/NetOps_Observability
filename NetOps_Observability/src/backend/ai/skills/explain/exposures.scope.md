---
topic: exposures.scope
question: Current verdicts or full history?
keywords: current verdicts, full history, superseded verdict, verdict scope
---
Current shows the latest verdict per check: one row per thing being checked, as
it stands now. Full history shows every recorded verdict including superseded
ones, so a check that failed last week and passes today appears twice. Use
current to decide what to fix and history to show that something changed. A
history row is a past verdict, never a live one.
