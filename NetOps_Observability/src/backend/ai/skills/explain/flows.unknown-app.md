---
topic: flows.unknown-app
question: What is the Unknown application row?
keywords: unknown application, unknown bucket, uncatalogued, internal traffic, share with no name
---
Traffic whose far end no naming source claimed — uncatalogued or internal. It
is measured like every other row here, not missing: it carries its own bytes,
flows and share, and it ranks against named applications rather than being
swept into a footnote. A large unknown share usually means the address
catalogue does not cover your internal ranges yet, not that the traffic is
suspicious. The Share with no name tile above is the same measurement as a
percentage of the window.
