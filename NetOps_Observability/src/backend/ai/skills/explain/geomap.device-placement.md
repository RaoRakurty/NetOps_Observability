---
topic: geomap.device-placement
question: How do devices get onto the map?
keywords: device placement, device locations, site label, latitude longitude, unplaced devices, map bubble
---
A device placed by the Source of Truth inherits its site's coordinates — set
them on the site itself, under Automation then Source of Truth, not on the
device. For everything else, type a site label plus a decimal latitude and
longitude on the device row here. Devices sharing a label fold into one bubble
on the map. Devices with no placement are listed first so you can see what is
still missing.
