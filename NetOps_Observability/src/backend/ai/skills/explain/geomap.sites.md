---
topic: geomap.sites
question: How do I declare a site on the map?
keywords: sites, site coordinates, wgs 84, latitude longitude, site slug, import sites, seed sites
---
Declare a site with a decimal latitude and longitude (WGS 84). Then assign a
device to it from Infrastructure then Devices, using the Site column, or give
the device a "site" label matching the site's slug shown in the table. It folds
into that site's map bubble and inherits these coordinates. Leave coordinates
blank to register a site that is not on the map yet. Import bulk-seeds sites
from a CSV, JSON or GeoJSON file: it adds intent only, and live discovery stays
authoritative.
