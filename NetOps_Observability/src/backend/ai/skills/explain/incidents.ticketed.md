---
topic: incidents.ticketed
question: What does Ticketed mean here?
keywords: ticketed incidents, mirrored to itsm
---
Ticketed on the Incidents page counts incidents mirrored into your ITSM and
acknowledged by it. Mirroring is optional and configured under Administration
→ integrations; with no integration the count stays at zero and nothing is
wrong. A push that errored is not counted here — it appears as a sync failure
on the Command Center.
