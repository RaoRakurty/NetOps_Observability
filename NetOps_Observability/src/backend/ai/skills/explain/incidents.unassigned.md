---
topic: incidents.unassigned
question: Why does an incident have no owner?
keywords: unassigned incidents, no owner
---
Unassigned on the Incidents page counts incidents with nobody named as owner.
An unowned incident is the one most likely to survive a shift change
untouched, which is why it is counted separately. Assign an owner from the
incident itself; assignment also decides who appears in the handoff summary.
