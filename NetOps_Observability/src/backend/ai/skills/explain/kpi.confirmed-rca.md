---
topic: kpi.confirmed-rca
question: What makes an RCA confirmed?
keywords: confirmed rca, confirmed verdict, confirmed impact
---
An RCA is confirmed on the Command Center when at least two independent
evidence streams agree on the same cause — for example device telemetry and
syslog, or flows and a path measurement. One stream alone is never enough to
confirm; that stays Suspected. Confirmed is the bar for opening a ticket and
escalating, because it is the point at which customer impact is supported by
evidence rather than inferred from a single source.
