---
topic: kpi.critical
question: What makes an incident critical here?
keywords: critical incident, critical severity, crit
---
On the Command Center an incident is critical when its impact is confirmed or
its blast radius is wide — many devices, a whole site, or a service path
customers ride. Severity is derived from the correlated group, not from the
loudest single alert inside it, so one screaming trap on a lab switch does not
become a critical. Selecting the number filters the queue to those rows.
