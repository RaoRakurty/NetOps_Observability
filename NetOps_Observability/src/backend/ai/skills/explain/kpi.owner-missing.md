---
topic: kpi.owner-missing
question: Why does an incident have no owner?
keywords: owner missing, no owner, unassigned incident
---
Owner missing on the Command Center means nobody has been assigned this
incident and no automatic assignment matched it. An unowned incident is the
one most likely to sit untouched through a shift change, which is why it has
its own number here. Assign from the row's Open RCA view. Owner
recommendations, where the platform can make one, appear on the incident
itself.
