---
topic: kpi.suspected-rca
question: What does Suspected RCA mean?
keywords: suspected rca, suspected verdict, impact not confirmed
---
Suspected on the Command Center means one evidence stream points at a cause
and nothing has corroborated it. It is a lead, not a finding. Suspected
incidents are deliberately not eligible for ticketing: opening a ticket on a
single stream sends someone to a device that may be fine. Confirm impact with
a second, independent source — then the verdict becomes Confirmed and the
ticket follows.
