---
topic: kpi.ticketed
question: What does the Ticketed number compare?
keywords: ticketed, ticket count, itsm sync
---
The Ticketed tile on the Command Center reads as opened over needed: incidents
already synced to your ITSM against confirmed incidents that still have no
ticket. Tickets are opened at the correlated-incident level, never per raw
alert, so one outage is one ticket. Selecting it opens the integrations page,
where the sync itself is configured and its failures are shown.
