---
topic: kpi.ticketed
question: What does the Ticketed number compare?
keywords: ticketed, ticket count, itsm sync
---
The Ticketed tile on the Command Center reads as filed out of all that need
one: open problems with a ticket in your ITSM, PagerDuty or chat tool, out of
those plus the confirmed problems that still have none. It reads the same
ticket links as the Ticket column, so the two always agree. Tickets are opened at the correlated-incident level, never per raw
alert, so one outage is one ticket. Selecting it opens the integrations page,
where the sync itself is configured and its failures are shown.
