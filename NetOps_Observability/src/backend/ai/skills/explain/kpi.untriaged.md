---
topic: kpi.untriaged
question: What does Untriaged mean?
keywords: untriaged, not yet triaged, rca not run
---
Untriaged on the Command Center means the signals have been correlated into an
incident but root-cause analysis has not produced a verdict yet: the group is
new, still running, or was never picked up. It is not a judgement about
severity. These are the rows where a human deciding what matters still adds
the most, because the engine has not yet said anything about cause.
