---
topic: lane.assessed-nothing
question: The scan assessed no device — what does that mean?
keywords: assessed no device, scan measured nothing, zero devices assessed
---
A scan that assessed no device measured nothing, so it is not a clear estate.
The run completed; it simply had nothing in scope it could reach. Check that
devices exist in the inventory, that the lane's scope includes them, and that
credentials and reachability let it read them. Findings published will stay at
zero until at least one device is actually assessed.
