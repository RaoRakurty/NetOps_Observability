---
topic: lane.security-lane
question: What does the security lane do?
keywords: security lane, assessment schedule, findings published, scan now
---
The security lane is the process that assesses every device in scope on a
schedule and publishes what it finds onto the platform's event bus, up to a
per-tenant cap of findings per run. Everything on the Security pages is
downstream of it. "Scan now" assesses your own tenant immediately instead of
waiting for the next interval; the result lands on the row below when it
finishes. The panel exists because a page that draws a funnel from a lane it
cannot see is reporting a measurement it has no evidence for.
