---
topic: licence.ceilings
question: What is a licence ceiling?
keywords: ceiling, licensed limit, soft limit, enforced limit
---
A limit the licence sets on something countable — monitored devices,
retention, and so on — shown against what is in use now. An enforced ceiling
gates: for devices, the ones past it stay in the inventory but are not
collected from. A soft ceiling records the overage and blocks nothing, so a
soft row at 96 % is a billing fact, not a fault. A row marked not enforced
carries a number the product does not currently gate on at all.
