---
topic: licence.ceilings
question: What is a licence ceiling?
keywords: ceiling, licensed limit, soft limit, enforced limit
---
A limit the licence sets on something countable — monitored devices,
retention, and so on — shown against what is in use now. An enforced ceiling
gates: past it, the next one is refused. A soft ceiling records the overage
and blocks nothing, so a soft row at 96 % is a billing fact, not a fault. A
row marked not enforced carries a number the product does not currently gate
on at all.
