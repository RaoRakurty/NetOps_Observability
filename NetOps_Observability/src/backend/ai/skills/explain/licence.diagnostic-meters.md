---
topic: licence.diagnostic-meters
question: Are diagnostic meters charged for?
keywords: diagnostic meters, not charged, telemetry not metered for money
---
No. They are recorded because they are useful — they tell you what this
installation is actually doing — but nothing on this list is billed. Telemetry
you run yourself is not metered for money. The entitlement meters above are
the ones that relate to what the licence covers; these sit beside them so byte
counts and device counts are never confused for each other.
