---
topic: licence.features
question: How do I read the capability table?
keywords: features, capabilities, included in tier, commercial capability
---
Each row is a commercial capability. "This licence" says whether the licence
in force grants it, and the last column names the lowest tier that includes it
— so a capability you do not have is priced, not hidden. A capability with no
tier stated is one the platform did not describe; that is a gap in the licence
document, not a statement that nothing grants it.
