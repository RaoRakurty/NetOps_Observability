---
topic: licence.independent-verification
question: How can I verify the licence myself?
keywords: verify licence, signed document, public key, offline check
---
A licence is a signed document, so it can be checked without this page and
without us. The public keys this build trusts are listed here and can be
downloaded; the recipe beside them uses nothing but the licence file and one
of those keys. That is the point of publishing them: a customer should be able
to prove what they were sold without asking the seller.
