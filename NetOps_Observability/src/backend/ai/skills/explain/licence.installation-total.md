topic: licence.installation-total
question: Why is the installation total higher than the tenants?
keywords: installation total, devices with no tenant, tenant lines add up to less
---
The installation line counts every monitored device on this installation,
including devices that belong to no tenant at all. The tenant lines below
count only devices attributed to a tenant. So the tenant lines can add up to
less than the installation total, and the difference is real devices, not a
rounding error — it is the unattributed ones.
