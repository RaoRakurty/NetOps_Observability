---
topic: licence.limit-not-a-fault
question: Is a licence limit a fault?
keywords: licence limit, 402, not a fault, nothing removed
---
No. Nothing has been removed, nothing has stopped collecting, and your data is
exactly where it was. What you are seeing is a limit on what this licence
covers — a commercial boundary, not a failure. The card names which limit,
where you stand against it, and which tier removes it. Everything already
inside the limit keeps working unchanged.
