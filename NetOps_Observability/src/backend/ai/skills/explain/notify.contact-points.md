---
topic: notify.contact-points
question: What is a contact point?
keywords: contact point, delivery audience, reports, email group
---
A reusable, named audience that scheduled Reports deliver to: an email group,
a Slack destination or a webhook. It exists so a report names "NOC on-call"
instead of carrying a list of addresses that has to be edited in five places.
Contact points are scoped to your tenant. They are not the alert channels
above: those are platform-global and carry alerts, these carry reports.
