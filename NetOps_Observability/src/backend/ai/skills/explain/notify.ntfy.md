---
topic: notify.ntfy
question: How does ntfy push work?
keywords: ntfy, push notification, topic, subscribe, free
---
The platform publishes to a topic on an ntfy server — the public ntfy.sh or
your own self-hosted one — and anyone subscribed to that topic in the ntfy app
receives the push. There is no account and no per-message cost, which makes it
the easiest way to prove the alert-delivery chain works end to end. Anyone who
knows a public topic name can subscribe to it, so use a long, unguessable
topic or a protected one with a token.
