---
topic: notify.twilio
question: What does SMS via Twilio cost?
keywords: twilio, sms, metered, critical alerts
---
Twilio is a metered third-party service — every message the platform sends is
billed to your own Twilio account, at your own rates. That is why it is
usually reserved for critical alerts and for the people who must be woken. For
testing the delivery path itself, use ntfy push instead: it is free and needs
no account.
