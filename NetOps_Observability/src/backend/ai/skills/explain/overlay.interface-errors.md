---
topic: overlay.interface-errors
question: What does the interface-errors overlay show?
keywords: interface errors overlay, discards, error rate, troubled links
---
Edges thicken and redden with the error and discard rate counted on their
interfaces. It answers a different question from utilization: a link can be
almost idle and still be dropping frames, and that link is the one worth
looking at. Errors here are rates, not totals, so a counter that stopped rising
fades rather than staying red forever.
