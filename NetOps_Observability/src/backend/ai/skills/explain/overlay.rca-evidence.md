---
topic: overlay.rca-evidence
question: What does the RCA-evidence overlay show?
keywords: rca evidence overlay, evidence used by rca, correlation evidence
---
Thick accent edges and marked nodes are the objects a root-cause correlation
actually used to reach its verdict — not everything that was unhealthy at the
time. It is the audit trail of one conclusion drawn on the map, so you can see
whether the engine reasoned over the part of the network you would have looked
at. Objects it did not use stay in their normal styling.
