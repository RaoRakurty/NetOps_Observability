---
topic: overlay.syslog
question: What does the syslog overlay show?
keywords: syslog overlay, device log activity, syslog in the window
---
Nodes are flagged where devices logged in the selected window. It shows where a
device had something to say, not whether what it said was serious — a chatty
device and a failing one look the same here until you read the messages.
Silence is likewise ambiguous: a device that sends no syslog may be healthy, or
may not be shipping logs at all.
