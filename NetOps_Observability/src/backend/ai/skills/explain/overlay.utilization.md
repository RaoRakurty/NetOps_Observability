---
topic: overlay.utilization
question: What does the utilization overlay show?
keywords: utilization overlay, link utilization, edge width, busy links
---
Edge width scales with measured link utilization, from zero to a hundred
per cent of the interface's own speed, so the busiest paths are the thickest
lines on the map. Links with no utilization measurement stay at their base
width rather than drawing as idle — an unmeasured link is not a quiet one. Use
it to find where traffic actually concentrates before reading capacity
numbers.
