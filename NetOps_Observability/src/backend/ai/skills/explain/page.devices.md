topic: page.devices
question: What is on the Inventory & Devices page?
keywords: device inventory, inventory and devices, devices page
---
Inventory & Devices lists every device the platform has discovered or you have
declared. Every device with an address is monitored, up to your licence limit;
any past it are marked over the limit and a banner says how many. Health here
comes from the device's own heartbeat combined with its correlated alerts, and
each row opens the device's full detail — interfaces, neighbours,
configuration and telemetry.
