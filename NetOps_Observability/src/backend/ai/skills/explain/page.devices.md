topic: page.devices
question: What is on the Inventory & Devices page?
keywords: device inventory, inventory and devices, devices page
---
Inventory & Devices lists every device the platform has discovered or you have
declared. Discovery is free and unlimited; the licence counts only the devices
you switch monitoring on for. Health here comes from the device's own
heartbeat combined with its correlated alerts, and each row opens the device's
full detail — interfaces, neighbours, configuration and telemetry.
