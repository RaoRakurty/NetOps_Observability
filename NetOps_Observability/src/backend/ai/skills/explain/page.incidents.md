topic: page.incidents
question: What is an operational incident?
keywords: operational incidents, incident record, incident lifecycle
---
An operational incident is a tracked problem opened from deduplicated alerts
and driven through a lifecycle — open, acknowledged, investigating, resolved —
with an owner and, optionally, a mirrored ticket in your ITSM. It is the
record of the work. Correlation groups on the Command Center are the engine's
view of cause; an incident is the human process wrapped around it.
