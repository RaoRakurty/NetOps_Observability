---
topic: paths.site-filter
question: What does the site filter on the Paths page do?
keywords: paths site filter, filter by site, site choice remembered
---
It narrows every Paths tab to one site: a tunnel with either end there, a WAN
interface on a router there, or a measured path whose target is there. A site's
name and its short form match each other, so "DC East" and "dc-east" are one
site. The list shows the sites the open tab has rows for. Rows with no site
appear only under All sites. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
