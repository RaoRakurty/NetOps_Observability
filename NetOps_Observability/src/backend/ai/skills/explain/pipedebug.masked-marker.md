---
topic: pipedebug.masked-marker
question: Why is the armed text not shown back?
keywords: masked marker, never shown in full, first characters and length
---
Because whatever you arm may itself be sensitive — a hostname, a session id, a
fragment of a message. The page shows only its first characters and its
length, which is enough to confirm you armed what you meant, and not enough to
recover it from a screenshot or a shared session.
