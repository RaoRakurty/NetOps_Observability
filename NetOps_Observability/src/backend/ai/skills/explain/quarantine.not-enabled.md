---
topic: quarantine.not-enabled
question: What does no quarantine stage mean?
keywords: sealing custody off, no quarantine stage, different from empty
---
Sealing custody is not enabled on this deployment, so unattributable events
are not sealed and held at all and this index does not exist. That is a
different fact from a quarantine that is empty: an empty quarantine means
everything resolved to a device, while no stage means nothing was ever
checked.
