---
topic: quarantine.nothing-held
question: What does nothing held mean?
keywords: nothing held, empty quarantine, every event resolved
---
Every event on the syslog, trap and flow lanes resolved to a device in the
inventory, so nothing needed sealing. A steady trickle here is normal while a
new device converges. A depth that keeps growing means something is sending
telemetry that nothing in the inventory owns — find the source address on the
row and assign the device.
