---
topic: queue.action-queue
question: What is in the Action Queue?
keywords: action queue, triage queue, what to work next
---
The Action Queue lists correlated incidents in the order they should be
worked: severity first, then age. Each row is a group of signals that share a
cause, not a single alert, and carries its own verdict, owner, blast radius
and next action. Opening a row shows the impacted entities, what correlated
and what to do. The same queue has its own full-height page under Operations.
