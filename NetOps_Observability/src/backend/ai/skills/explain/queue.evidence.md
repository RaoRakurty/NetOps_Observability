---
topic: queue.evidence
question: What does the evidence brief show?
keywords: evidence brief, correlated evidence, evidence ledger
---
The evidence brief on an expanded Action Queue row summarises what the engine
actually read: how many signals correlated, across how many nodes, the lead
reason they were grouped, and which signal domains contributed. Anything
expected but absent is listed as missing. Opening it takes you to the full
evidence ledger in the RCA view, where every item can be inspected.
