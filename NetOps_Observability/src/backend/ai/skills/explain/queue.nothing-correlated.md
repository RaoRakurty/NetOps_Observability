---
topic: queue.nothing-correlated
question: Why is the queue empty?
keywords: empty queue, nothing correlated, no incidents
---
An empty Action Queue means no group of signals has correlated into an
incident that needs action. It is not the same as no alerts: individual alerts
can be firing while nothing has yet grouped into a cause. Check Active Alerts
for raw firing conditions, and Data Sources if you expect signals that are not
arriving at all.
