---
topic: rca.candidates
question: What is an RCA candidate?
keywords: rca candidate, candidates, correlation group, evidence linked, last 24h
---
A candidate is one evidence-linked correlation group from the last 24 hours —
signals the engine believes share a cause, not a list of raw alerts. Each
carries a verdict: confirmed when two independent sources agree, suspected
when only one does, not confirmed while evidence is still arriving. The weaker
candidates are deliberately kept on screen and say exactly what is missing,
because a gap in collection is something you can fix; a hidden candidate is
not. The count in the header is the true window total, which can exceed the
rows loaded below.
