topic: rca.confidence-ladder
question: What do the steps on the confidence ladder mean?
keywords: confidence ladder, detected suspected probable confirmed, independent evidence, localized
---
Four steps, and the case only climbs one when evidence earns it. Detected means
an anomaly fired. Suspected means it was localized to a device or an area.
Probable means independent network evidence aligned — a second stream agreeing
with the first, not the same stream counted twice. Confirmed means customer
impact is supported by evidence rather than inferred, which is the bar for
opening a ticket. A locked step is a step the evidence did not reach; the
caption under it says what is still missing. Confirmation is also held back
when the evidence came from parser rules nobody has validated yet.
