topic: rca.failure-propagation
question: How do I read the failure-propagation ladder?
keywords: how it propagated, cascade, witnessed stage, dimmed stage, likely origin
---
A failure at the highlighted origin propagates downward: each stage is
something that broke because the stage above it did. A stage drawn solid was
witnessed — it lists the evidence that saw it, and the origin carries the
"Likely origin" mark. A dimmed stage is part of the known propagation path but
has no evidence in this window, so the workspace shows it and does not claim
it. That is the point of the ladder: it shows the shape of the cascade
including the rungs nobody observed, rather than quietly dropping them and
implying a cleaner story than the data supports.
