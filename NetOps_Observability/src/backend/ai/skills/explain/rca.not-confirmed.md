---
topic: rca.not-confirmed
question: What does Not confirmed mean on RCA Candidates?
keywords: not confirmed, undetermined, gathering evidence, no verdict yet
---
Not confirmed means the engine has grouped the signals but no evidence stream
has settled a cause yet. It is not a verdict of "nothing wrong" and not a
verdict of "fine" — it is the honest state of an analysis still gathering
evidence. Open the candidate to see which streams it is waiting on. If a
stream never arrives, the row stays here: the engine will not guess past a
gap. Fixing the missing collection is usually what turns one of these into a
suspected or confirmed root cause.
