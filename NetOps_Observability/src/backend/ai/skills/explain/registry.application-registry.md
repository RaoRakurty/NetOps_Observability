---
topic: registry.application-registry
question: What is the application registry for?
keywords: application registry, business application, owning team, accountability
---
It names the business applications this tenant runs and the team answerable
for each one. That is its whole job: ownership with a name, in the product,
rather than in a spreadsheet nobody can find at 3am. It does not group traffic
and it is not joined to the service catalog. Archiving keeps the record and
removes it from the active list.
