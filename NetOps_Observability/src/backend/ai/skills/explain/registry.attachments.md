---
topic: registry.attachments
question: What is an attachment?
keywords: attachment, binding, probe path seam, reference not measurement
---
A reference saying this service is measured through that probe, path or seam.
It is a reference, not a measurement: attaching one does not start anything,
and removing one stops the association without deleting the probe or the path
it names. Attachments are what let a service page show the measurements that
already exist for it.
