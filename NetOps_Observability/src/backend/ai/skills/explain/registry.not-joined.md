---
topic: registry.not-joined
question: Are the service catalog and application registry linked?
keywords: not joined, separate lists, service attached to application
---
No. They are separate lists today: a service cannot be attached to an
application in the product, and nothing enforces a relationship between them.
Shared names between the two are a convention you keep, not a link the
platform maintains — so do not read a matching name as a guarantee that the
same team owns both.
