---
topic: registry.service-catalog
question: What is a service here?
keywords: service catalog, operable unit of traffic, selector, attribution
---
An operable unit of traffic — the thing your team runs and is paged about. Its
value comes from its grouping rule: without a usable one the service is
carried in the list but nothing is attributed to it, and per-service flow
totals read as not measured rather than as idle. Archiving a service keeps its
history and stops it appearing in new attribution.
