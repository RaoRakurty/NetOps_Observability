---
topic: registry.which-drives-what
question: What does each registry drive?
keywords: registries, service catalog, application registry, cloud business services
---
Three separate lists. The service catalog is an operable unit of traffic; its
grouping rule is what makes per-service flow totals add up, and Explore →
Flows → Services reads those totals. The application registry names a business
application and the team accountable for it — it names ownership and does not
group traffic. Cloud business services is the cloud-side registry behind
resource assignment and the criticality-aware impact view.
