---
topic: roles.builtin
question: What are the standard roles?
keywords: built-in roles, standard roles, fixed roles, role matrix
---
The roles that ship with the platform. Each cell in the matrix shows what that
role may do in one product area: none, read, write or admin. They are fixed —
you cannot change what a standard role can reach, because other parts of the
product reason about them. If none of them fits, create a custom role instead
and set its cells yourself.
