---
topic: roles.custom
question: What is a custom role?
keywords: custom role, define a role, permission cell, access level
---
A role you define. Click a cell to cycle that product area between none, read,
write and admin; the change is saved as soon as you click. A custom role can
be assigned to users exactly like a standard one, and can be deleted when
nothing uses it. Custom roles are platform-wide definitions today — the same
role list is offered in every tenant.
