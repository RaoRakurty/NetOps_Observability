---
topic: roles.matrix
question: How do I read the role matrix?
keywords: role matrix, permissions grid, access level cell
---
Each row is a role and each column a product area. The cell says the highest
thing that role may do there: none, read, write or admin. Built-in roles are
fixed and their cells are not clickable. On a custom role, clicking a cell
moves it to the next level and saves immediately. A user's effective access is
their role's row, narrowed by the tenant they belong to.
