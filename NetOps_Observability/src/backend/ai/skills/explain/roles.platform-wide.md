---
topic: roles.platform-wide
question: Why are these roles shown but not editable?
keywords: platform-wide roles, tenant roles, role definitions shared
---
Role definitions are platform-wide today: one list of roles is offered to
every tenant, so editing them inside a tenant would change what they mean
everywhere. They are shown here for reference. To give this tenant's people
access, assign one of these roles to them under Users. Per-tenant role
definitions are a backend change that has not been made.
