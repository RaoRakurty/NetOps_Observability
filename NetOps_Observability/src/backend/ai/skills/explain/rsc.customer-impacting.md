---
topic: rsc.customer-impacting
question: Which incidents are counted on the scorecard?
keywords: customer-impacting incidents, internal platform events, include internal, scorecard scope
---
Customer-impacting incidents in the selected window. Internal and platform
self-monitoring is excluded unless you explicitly include it with the
"Include internal/platform events" checkbox on the NOC Recovery Scorecard.
That default exists so the numbers describe what your customers felt, not what
Correlix noticed about itself. On a very large window the read is capped: the
footnote names the number of most-recent incidents that were scanned, so a
capped window is never presented as a complete one.
