---
topic: rsc.mtbf
question: What does the repeat failure interval mean?
keywords: mtbf, mean time between failures, repeat interval, recurring failure
---
MTBF — Mean Time Between Failures — is the average time between repeat
incidents for the same object or domain on the NOC Recovery Scorecard. A short
interval means something is failing again before anyone finished dealing with
the last time it failed; that is the signature of a workaround rather than a
fix. The card reads "No repeats yet" when nothing in the window failed twice,
which is a fact about the window and not a claim that the estate is healthy.
The Recurring failure sources table names the specific objects behind it.
