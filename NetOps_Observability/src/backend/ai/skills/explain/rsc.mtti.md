topic: rsc.mtti
question: What is MTTI and why is it the hero metric?
keywords: mtti, time to isolate, root domain isolation, median isolation time, p50
---
MTTI — Median (or Mean) Time To Isolate — is the time from incident detection
to an evidence-backed root domain, seam and owner. It is Correlix's hero metric
on the NOC Recovery Scorecard: not a universal industry standard, but the one
number that shrinks the NOC investigation window, because it measures how long
people spent asking "whose problem is this?" before anyone could start fixing
it. P50 is the median — the normal case, half of incidents were isolated
faster. Isolation is not repair: the clock stops when the owner is known, not
when service returns.
