---
topic: rsc.recovery-readiness
question: How is Recovery Readiness scored?
keywords: recovery readiness, readiness score, primary drag, at risk stable healthy
---
A deterministic score out of 100 on the NOC Recovery Scorecard, computed from
four inputs: the repeat-incident rate, how much evidence coverage is connected,
long-tail (P90) isolation time, and the number of chronic offenders. Nothing is
modelled or predicted — the same window always produces the same score. The
state reads Healthy, Stable or At Risk, and "Primary drag" names whichever of
the four inputs is costing the most, so the score comes with the thing to fix
rather than just a grade.
