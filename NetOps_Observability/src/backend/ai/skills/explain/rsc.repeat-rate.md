---
topic: rsc.repeat-rate
question: What counts as a repeat-affected incident?
keywords: repeat rate, repeat affected incidents, chronic object, failed more than once
---
The percentage of incidents in this window associated with an object, path,
device or domain that has failed more than once. It answers "how much of the
NOC's time went to things we have already seen?" A high repeat rate is not a
detection problem; it means fixes are not sticking, or the fix belongs to
someone who has not been engaged. The Recurring failure sources table on the
same page lists the specific offenders and a recommended action for each.
