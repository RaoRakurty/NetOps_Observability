---
topic: rules.disabled
question: What happens when I disable a rule?
keywords: disable a rule, no rules registered, nothing evaluated
---
Disabling a rule silences its evidence everywhere, including the exposure
stories it would have grounded — the correlation engine never sees what it did
not produce. With no rule enabled nothing is being evaluated, so an empty
detections list would mean "not looked at", not "nothing found". Enable the
families you care about and read the detections list again.
