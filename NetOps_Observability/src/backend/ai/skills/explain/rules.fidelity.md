---
topic: rules.fidelity
question: What does rule fidelity mean?
keywords: fidelity, rule confidence, match confidence, not severity
---
Fidelity is the rule author's confidence that a match is really the thing the
rule names — how likely it is to be a true positive. It is not the severity of
what it finds: a high-fidelity rule can match something harmless, and a
low-fidelity rule can be the first sign of a serious problem. Use fidelity to
decide how much a match is worth acting on alone, and severity to decide how
fast.
