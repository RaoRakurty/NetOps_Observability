---
topic: seam.exposure
question: What is exposure by seam?
keywords: seam exposure, untrusted network boundary, seam owner, unassessed seam
---
A seam is one ownership handoff — where this estate meets a network somebody
else runs, such as an ISP circuit or a partner link. This strip attributes open
findings to the seam they sit on and names who owns each one, so a fix has an
owner rather than a queue. A seam nobody has assessed shows a dash, never a
zero: no finding on an unassessed seam means unknown, not clear.
