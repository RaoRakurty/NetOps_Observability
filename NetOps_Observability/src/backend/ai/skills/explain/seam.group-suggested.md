---
topic: seam.group-suggested
question: What is a proposed grouping?
keywords: suggested group, proposed grouping, not confirmed, confidence
---
A suggested group is the engine's proposal, not a settled fact: it saw evidence
that two seams carry the same traffic and wrote it down for a person to
confirm. It is labelled with what proposed it and how confident that was, and a
proposal with no stated confidence says so rather than showing a zero. Nothing
acts on a suggestion until someone confirms it.
