---
topic: seam.registry-unavailable
question: Why are seam groups unavailable?
keywords: seam registry unavailable, seam store not deployed, grouping unavailable
---
The seam registry is not present on this deployment, so seams are neither
grouped nor tracked here. That is not the same as an estate with no redundant
seams — nobody is looking. Seam attribution on findings still works where the
producer supplied it; only the grouping surface is absent.
