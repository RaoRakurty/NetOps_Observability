---
topic: sec.coverage
question: What does assessment coverage mean?
keywords: assessment coverage, coverage percentage, unassessed assets, estate assessed
---
Coverage is the share of the estate a security check actually looked at, not a
score. The Security Overview leads with it because every other number on the
page is only about what was measured. An asset nobody assessed is unknown, not
safe: the absence of a finding on it proves nothing, so it is drawn in the
neutral ramp and never as a green zero. Raise coverage by giving the lane
reachable credentials and a device list, then read the funnel again.
