---
topic: sec.lane-no-producer
question: Why is this evidence lane empty?
keywords: empty lane, no producer reporting, no verdicts yet
---
Each lane on the overview is fed by a producer: hardening and exposure verdicts
come from the security lane's own scans, threat detections come from the rule
engine over device logs. An empty lane means no producer reported into it, so
there is nothing to show. It is not a statement that the lane is clean. The
Security lane panel below says whether the producer ran, when, and whether what
it produced reached the engine.
