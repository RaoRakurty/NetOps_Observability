---
topic: sec.no-stories
question: Why are there no exposure stories?
keywords: no story grounded, nothing correlated, empty stories list
---
A story appears when security evidence lands on the same entity and seam as
other telemetry inside one correlation window. Nothing has met that bar yet, so
the engine has grounded no story. An empty list means nothing correlated — not
that nothing is wrong. Individual findings are still listed under Exposures,
and the Security lane panel says whether any evidence is being produced at all.
