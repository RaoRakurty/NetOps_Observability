---
topic: sec.standards-untagged
question: What is a standards tag?
keywords: standards tag, untagged finding, control set, framework tag
---
A standards tag maps a finding to a published control — a NIST 800-53 control,
a CIS control, a PCI requirement. Nothing can be reported against a control set
until findings carry one. The counts here are counts of tagged hardening
findings, not a framework compliance verdict: a framework score is computed in
Compliance from the controls in that framework's scope. Untagged findings are
still real findings; they just cannot roll up into a framework.
