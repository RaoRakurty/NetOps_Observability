---
topic: sec.trend-no-history
question: Why is there no verdict trend?
keywords: no assessment history, trend needs a scan, verdict trend empty
---
The trend plots fail, warning and pass counts per day from completed
assessments. With no completed scan in the selected range there is nothing to
plot — a trend cannot be drawn from a single point, and it is never drawn from
guesses. Run a scan, or widen the range to a period that contains one, and the
days appear.
