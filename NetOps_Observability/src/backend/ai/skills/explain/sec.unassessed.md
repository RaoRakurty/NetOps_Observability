---
topic: sec.unassessed
question: What does an unassessed verdict mean?
keywords: unassessed, no verdict, not applicable, check errored
---
Unassessed means the check ran but produced no verdict — it did not apply to
this asset, or it errored. It is not a pass. The platform keeps it neutral
rather than green so an operator never reads "we could not tell" as "this is
clear". Unassessed controls are counted in no passing share anywhere in
Compliance. The finding detail names the reason under "Why unassessed"; fix
that reason and the next scan produces a real verdict.
