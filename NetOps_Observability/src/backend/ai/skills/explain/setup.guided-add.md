---
topic: setup.guided-add
question: What does the guided Add do?
keywords: guided setup, add organization tenant user, nothing is created until create
---
One flow for the four things this page creates: an organization, a tenant, a
user, or an access grant. The first step picks which, and the remaining steps
adapt. Nothing is written until you press Create — leaving the wizard early
creates nothing at all. Everything it makes can be changed afterwards from the
Organizations tree, so no choice here is permanent.
