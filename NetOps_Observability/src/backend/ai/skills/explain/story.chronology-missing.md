---
topic: story.chronology-missing
question: Why is the chronology empty?
keywords: chronology not loaded, missing timeline, no causality chain
---
The hero shows the story's verdict from the story object itself; the ordered
chronology comes from a second read of the correlation timeline. When that read
does not return, the hero degrades to the summary rather than blanking. The
story is intact — open the full story and the causality path loads there. A
missing chronology is a failed read, never evidence that nothing happened.
