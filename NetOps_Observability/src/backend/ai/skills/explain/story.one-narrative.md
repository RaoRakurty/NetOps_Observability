---
topic: story.one-narrative
question: What is an exposure story?
keywords: exposure story, one narrative, correlated exposure, flagship story
---
An exposure story is one narrative instead of four alerts. The engine folded
several observations across several entities into a single story owned by the
seam they share, because they landed on the same entities inside one
correlation window. The counts beside it are what went in: observations are
the individual pieces of evidence, entities are the devices, interfaces and
seams they touched. Opening the story shows the same causality path, ownership
and export any network incident gets.
