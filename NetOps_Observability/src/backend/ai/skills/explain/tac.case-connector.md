---
topic: tac.case-connector
question: What is a case connector and why is none offered?
keywords: case connector, open the case, tac vendor portal, no connector, bundle download
---
A case connector is a configured link to a vendor's support system that lets
the Open the case step submit for you. When none is offered on this deployment,
nothing is broken and nothing is hidden: no connector is configured, so the
honest path is to download the redacted bundle and open the case in the
vendor's own portal yourself. A connector that exists but carries no
credentials is shown greyed with its own note instead. Correlix never opens a
case on its own in either situation — submitting is always a person's press.
