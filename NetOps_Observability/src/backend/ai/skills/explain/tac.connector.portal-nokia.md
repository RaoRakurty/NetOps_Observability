topic: tac.connector.portal-nokia
question: Why is Nokia a copy-and-paste path?
keywords: nokia tsr, nokia portal, nokia nsp apis, nokia support case
---
Nokia publishes no case API. NSP exposes exactly five APIs — NSP REST, RESTCONF,
Kafka, NFM-P REST and NFM-P XML — and none of them opens a TSR; the developer
portal returns nothing for ticket, support case or TSR, and the TSR guide has no
upload instruction at all. So Correlix prepares the case text and the redacted
bundle, and you open the case at customer.nokia.com. Nokia prefers the phone for
outages. Replying to the case email does work, but the subject line must not be
altered and the per-case reply address is never published, so Correlix cannot
compose that mail for you. Nokia publishes no severity scale, so none is
offered. Checked 2026-09-05.
