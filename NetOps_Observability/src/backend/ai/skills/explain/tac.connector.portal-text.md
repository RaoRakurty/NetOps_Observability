topic: tac.connector.portal-text
question: What is the Vendor portal / email path?
keywords: vendor portal, copy and paste case, portal text, no integration required
---
This is the path that always works and needs no integration at all. Correlix
pre-fills the case text from the evidence it collected and builds the redacted
bundle; you paste the text into the vendor's own portal or email and attach the
downloaded file yourself. It is the only supported path for vendors that publish
no case-creation API, and it is a sensible first move for any vendor before you
bring credentials. It opens nothing and attaches nothing on your behalf, so
nothing leaves Correlix until you send it. The bundle it prepares uses the
link-only profile: the case text and the manifest, with the outputs kept here
for download.
