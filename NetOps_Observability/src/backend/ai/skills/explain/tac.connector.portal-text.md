topic: tac.connector.portal-text
question: What is the Vendor portal or email path?
keywords: vendor portal, copy and paste case, portal text, no integration required
---
The path that always works and needs no integration. Correlix pre-fills the
case text from the evidence it collected and builds the redacted bundle; you
paste the text into the vendor's own portal or email and attach the file
yourself. It opens nothing and attaches nothing on your behalf, so nothing
leaves Correlix until you send it. It is chipped Manual for that reason. It also
steps aside: when your device's own vendor has a path that is ready, this row
moves behind "Show all connectors" rather than sitting beside it as a second,
slower way to do the same thing. Its bundle uses the link-only profile.
