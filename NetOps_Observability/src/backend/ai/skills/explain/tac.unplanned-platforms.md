---
topic: tac.unplanned-platforms
question: What is a platform with no plan?
keywords: unplanned platform, no authored plan, no command set, paste path, unplannable
---
Correlix recognises these platforms — it can tell you are running one — and has
authored no command set for them. Nothing is guessed to fill the hole. An
escalation on one of them says the platform carries no plan and offers the paste
path instead: you run the commands you trust, paste the output, and Correlix
reads it back through the parsers it does have. They are listed here rather than
omitted because a coverage page that hides the platforms it cannot plan for is
a marketing page. Authoring a plan for one is research work, filed under
ai/tac/research and merged by a human.
