---
topic: tenant.global-visibility
question: What does hiding a tenant from the global view do?
keywords: global visibility, hide tenant, operator restricted, data privacy
---
Global and platform-level users stop seeing this tenant's logs, flows,
findings and metrics. The tenant's own users are unaffected — nothing is
deleted and nothing stops collecting. Use it where data-privacy or compliance
rules say the provider must not read a customer's telemetry. A platform
operator who needs access anyway must open a break-glass session, which is
time-boxed and recorded.
