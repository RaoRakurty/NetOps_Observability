---
topic: tenant.one-at-a-time
question: Why must I choose a tenant before this page opens?
keywords: choose a tenant, tenant gate, one tenant at a time, merged telemetry, cross-tenant
---
You have platform-wide access, so this page could show every tenant at once —
but merged telemetry is unreadable and easy to misread: ten customers'
interface metrics and logs interleaved in one table invites reading one
tenant's incident with another tenant's data in view. So cross-tenant reach is
not cross-tenant display. You hold the keys to every door, but you open one
door at a time. Pick a tenant here and the whole app follows it — every page,
query and export is that tenant's until you switch. Administration and Iris are
never gated: that is where you manage tenants and choose which one to open.
