---
topic: threats.detection-not-verdict
question: Is a detection a verdict?
keywords: detection is evidence, not a verdict, grounds into correlation
---
A detection is evidence, not a verdict. One rule matching one log line says
something happened, not that it mattered. Detections ground into the
correlation engine and surface as exposure stories when they land on the same
entity and seam as other telemetry — that correlation, with a stated
confidence, is where a verdict comes from. Act on a single detection only when
you already know it is significant.
