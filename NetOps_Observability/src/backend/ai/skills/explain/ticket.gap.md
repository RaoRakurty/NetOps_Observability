---
topic: ticket.gap
question: What is the ticketing gap?
keywords: ticketing gap, ticket gap
---
The ticketing gap on the Command Center is the distance between what has been
confirmed and what has been recorded in your ITSM. It exists because tickets
are opened for correlated incidents, not for raw alerts: a gap means a real,
confirmed problem has no case number, not that an alert went unnoticed. The
four tiles below it split that gap into opened, needed, held and failed.
