---
topic: ticket.needed
question: Why is a ticket needed for this incident?
keywords: ticket needed, needs a ticket, unticketed
---
Ticket needed on the Command Center means the incident has a confirmed root
cause and no ticket has been opened for it. Confirmed is the bar on purpose:
it is the point at which customer impact rests on corroborated evidence rather
than on one source. These are the rows to open a case for before the shift
ends.
