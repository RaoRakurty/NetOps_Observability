---
topic: ticket.not-eligible
question: Why can this incident not be ticketed?
keywords: not eligible, ticket not eligible, hold
---
Not eligible on the Command Center means the incident's root cause is not
confirmed yet, so it is held back from ticketing. It is a hold, not a refusal:
as soon as a second independent evidence stream agrees, the verdict becomes
confirmed and the incident moves to Ticket needed. Ticketing a suspicion sends
someone to a device that may be healthy.
