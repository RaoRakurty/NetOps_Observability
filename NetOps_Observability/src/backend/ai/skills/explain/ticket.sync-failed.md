---
topic: ticket.sync-failed
question: What does Sync failed mean?
keywords: sync failed, itsm push failed, ticket error
---
Sync failed on the Command Center means the platform tried to open or update a
case in your ITSM and the request errored — bad credentials, a changed
endpoint, a rejected field. The incident is still tracked here; only its
mirror is missing. Selecting the tile opens the integrations page, where the
error and the retry live.
