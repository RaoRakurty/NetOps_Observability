---
topic: ticketing.outbox
question: What is the ticket delivery outbox?
keywords: ticket outbox, queued failed delivered, last error, audit trail
---
Everything auto-ticketing has queued, failed or delivered to the external
system, with the provider's own refusal text on the row that failed and every
recorded transition beside it. It is the place to tell "the policy never
fired" apart from "the row is queued behind a provider that is refusing us".
Policies and destinations themselves are configured under Ticketing &
Automation.
