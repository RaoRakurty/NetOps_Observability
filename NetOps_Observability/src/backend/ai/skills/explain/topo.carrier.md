---
topic: topo.carrier
question: What is the carrier overlay?
keywords: carrier overlay, transport network, shared carrier, carrier fabric
---
Carrier draws the shared transport network — the provider fabric that ties LAN,
SD-WAN, data centre and cloud together — on top of the devices you own. It is
an overlay, not new inventory: turning it off removes the transport lines and
leaves the same estate. Use it to see which of your sites ride the same carrier
segment, so one provider fault is read as one fault rather than as several
unrelated site problems.
