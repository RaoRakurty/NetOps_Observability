---
topic: topo.confidence
question: How is confidence in a link or node decided?
keywords: topology confidence, corroborated observation, single source observation, independent sources
---
Confidence counts independent observers. Two or more sources that saw the same
thing by different means is corroboration, and reads as confirmed. One source
is an observation that nothing has agreed with yet, and a one-way observation —
seen from one end only, with no reverse evidence — is weaker still. A high
score with no evidence records attached means the graph believes it but cannot
show you why. Nothing here is inferred upward: an object with no direct
evidence says so.
