---
topic: topo.domain
question: What does the Domain picker change?
keywords: network domain, all networks, sd-wan domain, dc domain, cloud domain, topology domain
---
Domain filters the one canvas rather than opening a different map. All networks
is the whole discovered estate — on-prem fabric plus the projected cloud
network. SD-WAN keeps the WAN edge: gateways, tunnels and transport. DC keeps
the data-centre fabric: spine, leaf, top-of-rack and the compute rows. Cloud
keeps the discovered cloud network: VPCs and VNets, subnets, gateways and
network appliances. Nothing is added by switching; the other options only hide
part of what All networks already shows.
