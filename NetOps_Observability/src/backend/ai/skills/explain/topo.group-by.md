---
topic: topo.group-by
question: What does grouping the canvas by a dimension do?
keywords: group by, group dimension, zone grouping, site grouping, regroup canvas
---
Grouping collapses nodes that share a value into one card, so a large fabric
stays readable. Site, region, role, vendor and owner group by that attribute.
Zone groups by ownership border — LAN, WAN, data centre, cloud, ISP and direct
cloud interconnect — which is what separates the parts you operate from the
parts somebody else does. None draws every device flat. Grouping changes the
drawing only; no device is dropped, and expanding a group shows its members.
