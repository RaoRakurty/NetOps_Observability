topic: topo.inventory
question: What is the device rail beside the map?
keywords: device inventory rail, inventory panel, devices beside the map
---
The rail lists every device the canvas resolved, with its status, management
address and when it was first observed. Selection is two-way: picking a row
highlights the node, picking a node scrolls the row into view. The counts
describe the whole fleet, not the slice currently drawn, and the filter narrows
the list without changing that total. It is suppressed while the canvas is
aggregated at scale, because a list of thousands of rows is not the tool for
reaching one device — search is.
