---
topic: topo.sample-data
question: Why does the canvas say sample data?
keywords: sample data, sample fabric, not your network, demo topology
---
No live topology resolved for this view, so the canvas is drawing a bundled
example fabric instead of a blank screen. Nothing on it is your network: the
device names, links and health are fixtures. The badge is always present while
a sample is shown, and it disappears the moment a real view resolves. If it
persists, the topology read is returning nothing — check that discovery is
running and that this domain and time range have data.
