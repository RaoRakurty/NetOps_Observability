---
topic: topo.unresolved
question: What is an unresolved node?
keywords: unresolved node, unmanaged neighbour, unknown device on the map
---
Something on the wire announced itself and discovery could not match it to a
managed device. It is drawn as a dashed placeholder rather than dropped,
because a neighbour you cannot name is still a neighbour. The drawer shows what
it was discovered as, the closest suggested match and the evidence that is
missing to confirm one. Resolving it is usually a credential or a discovery
range problem, not a topology problem.
