question: Why does a workflow say it is not implemented?
keywords: workflow placeholder, prepared not implemented, later phase workflow
---
The canvas ships Explore, Investigate, Path trace and Dependency. The remaining
workflows are listed because their adapters exist and their data contracts are
settled, but nothing renders them yet. They are shown rather than hidden so the
set of intended workflows is honest, and selecting one says so instead of
drawing a map that would look like a result.
