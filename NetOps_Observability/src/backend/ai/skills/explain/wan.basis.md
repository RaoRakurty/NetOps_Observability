topic: wan.basis
question: Why is this interface counted as WAN?
keywords: why wan, wan basis, transport interface, possible wan, not confirmed, why is this wan
---
Each WAN interface shows the fact that made it one: the SD-WAN controller calls
it a transport port, the device lists it as a transport locator, a tunnel runs
over it, an ISP next-hop is declared for it, or an operator marked it. Those are
confirmed and measured. A possible interface has only a hint — a WAN-sounding
description, a public address, or a WAN router with nothing confirmed — so it is
shown apart and not measured until someone marks it as WAN or not WAN.
