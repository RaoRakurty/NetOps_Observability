---
topic: wifi.remediation-empty
question: What does an empty remediation queue mean?
keywords: nothing waiting, empty approval queue, no proposals
---
Nothing is waiting for a decision. Because a proposal is only raised from
evidence that took part in a confirmed incident, an empty queue means no
incident has met that bar — it is not a statement that the wireless estate is
healthy. Incidents that were never confirmed, and confirmed ones with no
eligible action, both leave this queue empty.
