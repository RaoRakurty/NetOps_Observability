---
topic: wifi.remediation-readonly
question: Why can I see proposals but not act on them?
keywords: remediation read only, infrastructure write access, cannot approve
---
Approving, rejecting and executing change a radio that is serving clients, so
they need write access to infrastructure. Read access shows you everything that
is proposed and everything that has been decided, which is what you need to
follow an incident. An administrator for this tenant can grant the write
permission.
