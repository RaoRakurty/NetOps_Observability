topic: wifi.uplink
question: Why does an access point show a switch port?
keywords: lan uplink, ap switch port, wireless to lan join, second witness
---
The switch port an access point plugs into is where wireless meets the wired
LAN, and it is the join the platform uses to reason across both. It also
supplies the second, independent witness a confirmed wireless verdict needs: a
radio complaint plus its own uplink port going down is one story, while a radio
complaint alone is one observer. An unknown uplink means the access point has
not been matched to a switch port yet.
